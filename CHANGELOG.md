# Changelog

All player-facing changes, newest first. This file is the only source: the game's What's new
page is built from it.

## [0.39.0] - 2026-11-14

### New
- Who is who: a page with every colleague you have met (what he does, what helps, the towers that answer him, and on which floors he turns up) and every tower (what it does and who it is good against). Colleagues you have not met yet are a silhouette. Open it from the career screen or the pause menu.
- The next wave is a row of faces with a count. Colleagues with a rule of their own come first, in the warning colour; new ones say NEW. Click one for his card.
- The list of who is on the floor shows the same small icons as over their heads.
- WHAT HURT YOU on the end screen shows the towers that answer each colleague, and says so when you had none of them.

## [0.38.0] - 2026-11-14

### Changed
- New towers now arrive next to the colleagues they answer. Coffee Corner brings Headphones and Office Artillery, the Presentation Room Quick Reply and Self-Service, the Canteen The Shredder and Thumbtacks.
- Self-Service has a verb of its own: please hold. Everyone in its small circle walks slower (60% of their speed), and it still answers them all now and then. Its PORTAL path gets Hold Music instead of Read the Manual: 45%.
- Noise Cancelling Headphones keep the Chatterbox from talking any tower in their range quiet, not just themselves.
- A new colleague comes in a calmer wave the first time: The Chaser and The Old Guard on Coffee Corner, The Impersonator in the Canteen.
- The Presentation Room: the big wave of Chasers is a little smaller (27 instead of 30).
- The Chaser's card now names Headphones, Quick Reply and Self-Service as answers.

## [0.37.0] - 2026-11-14

### Changed
- While placing a tower, the bit of walkway it would reach lights up yellow, and a label next to the mouse says how much: "Sees 32% of the walkway". This replaces the red floor for what it cannot see.
- On floors with a Chatterbox, a dotted red line along the walkway shows how far he talks while you place a tower. Build beyond it and he cannot silence it.
- A selected tower that aims shows the walkway it covers the same way.

## [0.36.0] - 2026-11-14

### New
- Under the shop, a strip says what the tower under your mouse does, which colleagues on this floor it is good against, and that every copy you own makes the next one dearer.
- Towers that arrive on this level say NEW in the shop until you put one down.
- Every upgrade button says what changes in numbers: "Damage 15 > 35", "Reload 3.2s > 2.2s", or NEW for something the tower did not do before.

### Changed
- Sell and Targeting sit at the top of the tower panel, right under the name, instead of falling off the bottom.
- Slow towers show their reload time ("Reload 3.2s") instead of 0.31 shots a second. "Damage per Coffee" left the panel; it stays on the end screen.
- "Shields" in the panel is now "Boxes": only the Old Guard's archive box and the Repair Guy's toolbox stop light hits. Other shields take anything.
- With more than eight towers the shop shows three smaller ones per row, so all fourteen fit on the screen.

## [0.35.0] - 2026-11-14

### Fixed
- Brown Noise now shows hidden colleagues until the next pulse. Before, it only worked for towers that happened to be built after the Headphones.
- A Motivational Poster or Headphones silenced by a Chatterbox no longer speed up the towers around them. "It does nothing until he moves on" now includes helping.
- Sharp Keycaps hurt the Old Guard through his box, as the keyboard promises.

### Changed
- Town Hall: the two Old Guards in wave 14 come in a little further apart.

## [0.34.0] - 2026-11-14

### New
- Small icons over colleagues who shrug something off: a crossed-out hand (PHYSICAL does nothing), a crossed-out laptop (DIGITAL does nothing), a box with "10" (small hits bounce off), a crossed-out eye (hidden) and an exclamation mark (pulls the attention of towers nearby). They can be switched off in Settings.
- A tower that sits next to colleagues it cannot hit says so: a grey icon over it, and a line in its panel saying who and why ("Cannot touch The Board Member", "Cannot see The Impersonator", "Something is in the way").

- The Chatterbox shows his circle: towers inside it stop working while he talks.
- If a colleague with a rule of his own reaches your desk and nothing on the floor answers him, a hint says what would. Once per colleague per round, and not when you are already losing.
- DIGITAL towers stand on a blue ring, PHYSICAL towers on an orange one, the same colours as in the shop.

### Changed
- "BLOCKED" is now "BOUNCED OFF", and only the first few hits on each colleague say it.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-08

### New
- Play together: host a game, give your colleagues the code or the link, up to 8 players.
- The host picks the floor, the number of players, and the Coffee: one shared wallet, or everyone their own (income is split between everyone, like Bloons co-op).

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-08

### Changed
- Tower upgrades now work like Bloons TD 6: every tower has three paths of four tiers. Take one path to tier 4 and a second to tier 2. The third stays shut.
- Auto-Reply: TEMPLATES (more per reply, a splash, bounces, and Inbox Zero, which takes half the HP of everyone in range every 8 seconds), RULES (faster, then several colleagues at once) and READ RECEIPTS (range, sees hidden colleagues, extra damage against bosses, and Legal Hold, which slows everyone in range).
- Coffee Machine: BREWING (more Coffee), EXPENSE CLAIM (Coffee goes into a pot with interest; you decide when to claim it, but a full pot stops growing) and VENDING (tip jars: colleagues dealt with nearby pay more).
- OVERTIME and ESCALATION are gone. Their ideas live on in the new paths.
- The tower panel shows all three paths, what the next tier does, and why a path is locked. A tower is named after its highest upgrade.
