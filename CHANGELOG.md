# Changelog

All player-facing changes, newest first. This file is the only source: the game's What's new
page is built from it.

## [0.17.1] - 2026-11-12

### Changed
- Reception reworked: a glass wall now runs across the whole hall, and the speed gates are the only way through. Everyone walks past the reception desk to sign in first, then through the gates to your desk on the office side. The lifts are behind the gates, where they belong.
- The coat rack in the middle of the hall is gone. A round coat stand waits by the door.
- Your desk faces the people coming for you: they walk up to the front of it, not to the back of your chair. At the edge of a floor it stands sideways.

## [0.17.0] - 2026-11-12

### New
- Level 1 is now Reception: your first day behind the reception desk. A marble hall with a glass front, waiting benches, lounge chairs, the lifts and a golden sculpture nobody understands. Same waves, same Head of Comms.
- The gates: everyone stops at the speed gates for a moment to scan their pass, then the glass flaps swing open. A good spot for your first Auto-Reply.
- Tall things block sight now on these floors: pillars, the logo wall, the lifts. Benches, tables and lounge chairs do not.

### Changed
- Coffee Corner is furnished like a real office kitchen: counters with the sink, the coffee machines and a hob, a long dining table with chairs, two standing tables and a bar by the window.
- In Coffee Corner you build on the worktops only: counters, tables and the bar. Your things stand on top, without their cabinet.

## [0.16.0] - 2026-11-08

### New
- Level 7, The Car Park: 15 waves weaving between parked cars and vans, past the ticket machine. Opens once you finish level 6.
- Exhaust fumes: every so often clouds drift across the car park. Towers standing in one reach a lot less far until it clears. Office Artillery does not care.
- Delegation: passes the problem on. One colleague gets it, then the next one near him, and every jump hits harder than the one before. ESCALATE makes the chain longer and harsher, DELEGATE passes it on faster, PROTOCOL widens the circle.
- The Phone Caller: always on the phone, and towers that only ever aim at one colleague drop everything for him. Those do him half damage; anything that hits a crowd does him in full.
- Boss: The Smoking Colleague. Towers near him reach less far, and he leaves clouds of smoke behind wherever he walks.
- The tower panel says when a tower stands in the smoke.

### Fixed
- A long note in the tower panel (quiet, mopped) could squeeze up and run over the upgrades below it.

## [0.15.0] - 2026-11-08

### New
- Level 6, Work From Home: the first medior floor. 15 waves on a spiral through your own house, with walls your towers cannot see through (Office Artillery lobs over them). Opens once you finish level 5.
- No Internet: every so often the connection drops and your WRITTEN towers send nothing. Run the dinosaur over the cacti until it reconnects and you get Coffee for it; leave it and it comes back by itself. Online everyone reconnects their own.
- Reply All: slow, but every email lands on everyone standing near the colleague it was meant for. URGENT hits harder, THREAD sends faster and to two at once, EVERYONE lands wider.
- The Cold Caller: he does not read email, he calls. Nothing WRITTEN touches him.
- Boss: The Baby. Every tower near him aims at him and nobody else, and every so often he has a crying fit that stops every tower close by. Noise Cancelling headphones do not hear it.
- The tower panel says why a tower is quiet: a Chatterbox, the Baby, or no internet.

## [0.14.0] - 2026-11-08

### New
- Somebody is typing at the next desk. Calm and barely there while all is well, faster and louder the less Focus you have left. Its own slider in Settings: Typing in the background.

### Changed
- The office sounds like an office: Coffee Machines bubble, replies whoosh off like sent mail, hits slap like paper, colleagues dealt with crumple up, a colleague at your desk lands with a thud, towers go down on a desk, upgrades chime, a new wave pings like a notification, and claiming a pot or getting Coffee from a colleague rings the till.

## [0.13.0] - 2026-11-08

### New
- Recognition shop, under Your career: spend what your stars earned on perks for your own game. Startup Budget (+15 Coffee on every floor), Extra Caffeine (+10 Focus), Bulk Discount (everything 10% cheaper) and Smoke Breaks. Online everyone starts the same.
- Smoke Break: a button in the top bar while you have one. +15 Focus in the middle of a floor, up to what you started with.
- Promotion: finish level 5 for the first time and you are a Medior. The medior floors are on their way.

## [0.12.0] - 2026-11-08

### New
- Level 5, Boardroom: 22 waves on a loop round the boardroom table, on a dark wood floor with a burgundy runner. Opens once you finish level 4.
- Specials: towers you get one of each. They come with their own three paths.
- Keyboard Smash: every few seconds it slams the walkway in front of the first colleague, hitting everyone around, and leaves the keyboard across the walkway. Nobody gets past until they have kicked it out of the way.
- Office Chair: rolls in a straight line, and you pick which way with the arrows in its panel. Every colleague it runs into takes damage, and the chair loses as much HP itself. Empty, or at the end of its lane, it rolls back and goes again.
- Boss: Your Line Manager. A stack of agenda points as a shield, then peer reviewers who keep coming, and in the end every tower near him reloads a lot slower.

### Fixed
- The sounds for tacks, mopping and the lunch bell that 0.6.0 promised were made but never played. They play now.

## [0.11.0] - 2026-11-08

### New
- Claim all: once you own an Offshore Account (Coffee Machine, EXPENSE CLAIM tier 4), a Claim all button sits next to your Coffee in the top bar. One click empties every pot of yours. C does the same when nothing is selected.

## [0.10.0] - 2026-11-08

## [0.9.1] - 2026-11-08

### Changed
- The projector mini-game shows a real projector now, with its VGA, HDMI and power ports on the side. Plug a cable in and it hangs out of the projector for real. The HDMI port is there to tempt you.

## [0.9.0] - 2026-11-08

### New
- Play together: a lobby setting for towers. By default everyone runs their own: only whoever built a tower can upgrade, sell or aim it. The host can switch to "anyone can use any tower". Selling a tower pays everyone back their own share of what they put in.
- Offshore Account (Coffee Machine, EXPENSE CLAIM tier 4): claiming it empties every pot of yours at once.

### Fixed
- The new Shields line in the tower panel squeezed the other labels. It is short now, with the explanation on hover.

## [0.8.0] - 2026-11-08

### Changed
- Headphones and The Shredder each do one thing now. Headphones stop everyone in range for a moment with every pulse, and no longer slow anyone. NOISE CANCELLING stops them for longer, VOLUME reaches further and pulses more often (Drop the Bass pushes colleagues back towards the door), FOCUS MUSIC makes stopped colleagues take more damage and speeds up your towers nearby.
- The Shredder no longer slows: it grinds down everyone inside (a little more damage than before) and reveals hidden colleagues. Only the PAPER JAM path slows, and Paper Trail keeps the slow going after the zone.
- Shields are a bright blue bar above the HP bar, visible from the start. The colleague card says how hard a hit must be to get through, and every tower panel says whether that tower breaks through shields or bounces off.
- A Go home button in the top bar. It asks first.
- What's new and How it works have a Back button at the top too.
- Two floors still to come have English names now: The Car Park and Hot Desks.

## [0.7.0] - 2026-11-08

### New
- Towers start to look like the path you chose, like in Bloons: from tier 3 every path has its own model. The Shredder is first: a red cross-cut machine with its blades on show, a jammed one that ends up Out of Order, or a locked confidential bin with a warning light. The other towers follow.

## [0.6.1] - 2026-11-08

### Changed
- The top bar shows which floor you are on (hover it for the intro).
- A settings button in the top bar, and Settings in the pause menu: volume, sound effects and the rest without leaving the game. Offline the game waits while it is open.
- Every tower with a range shows it while you place it or select it: Headphones, The Shredder and Thumbtacks too, not only the shooting towers.
- Office Artillery can now be told who to aim at, like every other tower that shoots.

## [0.6.0] - 2026-11-08

### New
- Level 4, Canteen: 22 waves along a long spiral round the tables, with wet floor where you cannot build. Opens once you finish level 3.
- Thumbtacks: drops piles of tacks on the walkway. Every tack hits one colleague, a pile stays until it runs out or gets old, and tacks do not need to see anyone. SHARPER hits harder, then gets through shields and turns piles into carpet. SUPPLY drops faster and more. HEALTH AND SAFETY reaches further, slows, stops colleagues for a moment and hurts bosses. You choose where the piles land: near your desk, near the door, close by, far out or anywhere.
- Self-Service: answers everyone in a small circle around it at once. KNOWLEDGE BASE hits harder, CHATBOT answers faster, PORTAL slows, finds hidden colleagues and pays Coffee.
- The Impersonator: nobody sees him coming. Towers cannot aim at him until a Shredder zone shows him up. Thumbtacks get him anyway.
- Lunch break: every so often a crowd of Interns heads for the canteen and everyone on the floor walks faster for a few seconds.
- Boss: The Cleaner. Everyone near him walks faster, he sweeps up your tacks, and any Shredder he walks into stops until the floor is dry.
- New sounds for tacks, mopping and the lunch bell.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-08

### New
- Level 3, Meeting Room: 21 waves along a walkway that zigzags around the meeting table. Opens once you finish level 2.
- Quick Reply: a cheap, fast tower. Every reply on the same colleague hits harder than the last, up to double. KEEP TYPING builds up further and keeps half of it when the target changes, SHORTCUTS replies faster and to two colleagues at once, EMOJI reaches further, sees hidden colleagues, hits bosses harder and finally slows whoever it hits.
- The projector breaks down now and then. Plug both cables into the right port, pick the right display setting, and you get Coffee for every second you had left. Until you do, you cannot build. Ignore it and it sorts itself out after 10 seconds. Online everyone fixes their own.
- Boss: The Presenter. Every few seconds he beams a slide straight at your desk: a handful of Chasers appear right next to it. His loading screen fills up again at every phase.

### Changed
- Office Artillery shots fly faster, so they land where the colleague still is.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-08

### New
- Level 2, Coffee Corner: 22 waves on long straights. Opens once you finish level 1.
- Headphones: every few seconds everyone in range slows right down. NOISE CANCELLING stuns (and cannot be talked quiet), VOLUME reaches further and slows all the time, FOCUS MUSIC makes every hit land harder and finally speeds up your towers nearby.
- Office Artillery: reaches the whole floor and breaks through shields. HEAVY DUTY hits harder and pins colleagues to the carpet, RAPID FIRE reloads faster and hits three at once, PRECISION sees hidden colleagues and pays Coffee.
- The Shredder: a zone that slows, reveals hidden colleagues and shares its damage over everyone inside. CROSS-CUT makes the damage stick after the zone, PAPER JAM stops everyone in it for a moment, CONFIDENTIAL pays Coffee for what it shreds.
- The Chatterbox: every tower near him stops working while he talks. Only Noise Cancelling headphones shut him up.
- The Old Guard: drags the archive box along. Light hits bounce off it, and the Shredder may not touch it.
- The Board Member: attends on a screen. Nothing in person touches him.
- Boss: The Repair Guy. No Coffee comes in at all while he is on the floor, and his toolbox stops light hits.
- Stunned colleagues show stars, silenced towers show a crossed-out speech bubble, and your Coffee counter turns red while income is blocked.

### Changed
- Nothing can hit a colleague who is still outside the door.

## [0.3.1] - 2026-11-08

### Changed
- New lobbies start with everyone their own Coffee (income split between everyone). The host can still switch to one shared wallet.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-08

### New
- Play together: host a game, give your colleagues the code or the link, up to 8 players.
- The host picks the floor, the number of players, and the Coffee: one shared wallet, or everyone their own (income is split between everyone, like Bloons co-op).
- The host also picks where people can build: anywhere, top and bottom, left and right, four quarters, or a patch each. In the lobby the host puts every player in their part.
- Every tower shows a ring in its owner's colour. With your own wallets, only the owner upgrades or sells a tower, and you can give Coffee to a colleague (the +10 button, Shift for 50).
- The host calls the waves, sets the speed and can pause or give up for everyone. Lost your connection? The game keeps going and lets you back into your seat.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-08

### Changed
- Tower upgrades now work like Bloons TD 6: every tower has three paths of four tiers. Take one path to tier 4 and a second to tier 2. The third stays shut.
- Auto-Reply: TEMPLATES (more per reply, a splash, bounces, and Inbox Zero, which takes half the HP of everyone in range every 8 seconds), RULES (faster, then several colleagues at once) and READ RECEIPTS (range, sees hidden colleagues, extra damage against bosses, and Legal Hold, which slows everyone in range).
- Coffee Machine: BREWING (more Coffee), EXPENSE CLAIM (Coffee goes into a pot with interest; you decide when to claim it, but a full pot stops growing) and VENDING (tip jars: colleagues dealt with nearby pay more).
- OVERTIME and ESCALATION are gone. Their ideas live on in the new paths.
- The tower panel shows all three paths, what the next tier does, and why a path is locked. A tower is named after its highest upgrade.
- Keys , . and / buy the next tier of path 1, 2 or 3, like in Bloons. C claims an Expense Claim pot.

### Balance
- Measured with bots: putting your Coffee into one tower's upgrades now clearly beats buying more copies. A first-time player still wins with some Focus lost; good play is flawless.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-07

### New
- CTRL-ALT-DEFEND is back, now in your browser and in 3D. No download, no installer.
- Level 1, Open-Plan Office: 21 waves and a boss.
- Two towers: Coffee Machine and Auto-Reply, each with three upgrade levels.
- Auto-Reply has two side paths: OVERTIME (faster, then more range) or ESCALATION (an extra target, then more damage). Pick one, the other locks.
- Four colleagues: The Intern, The New Hire, The Requester (splits into two Chasers when you deal with him) and The Chaser.
- Boss: The Head of Comms. His Interns walk in front of him and take your shots. Set a few towers to Most HP.
- Six targeting modes per tower: First, Last, Closest, Farthest, Most HP, Least HP.
- Speeds 1x, 2x, 4x and 8x, pause, and calling the next wave early for points.
- Every hit is visible: flying replies, damage numbers, BLOCKED and IMMUNE when something does not land.
- New colleagues introduce themselves the first time they show up, with what beats them.
- End screen with WHAT HURT YOU (sorted by Focus lost), what your towers did, and damage per Coffee.
- Give up takes you to the end screen with your numbers, not just back to the menu.
- Feedback after every round: how much fun it was (0 to 10) plus a comment, sent with one button.
